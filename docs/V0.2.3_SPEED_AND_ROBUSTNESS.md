# v0.2.3 speed and robustness validation

## Solver change

The constrained multiscale-Gamma EM loop is compiled with Numba and performs
the sufficient-statistic accumulation, constrained rate update, normalized
E-step, and convergence check without allocating full intermediate matrices on
every iteration. Numba is a required runtime dependency in 0.2.3.

The ordinary adaptive candidate search uses one split-warm initialization and
relative tolerance `1e-4`. High-order ambiguous fits retain a tighter guarded
confirmation. A second guard detects three or more numerically duplicate signal
profiles in models with at least 12 components and refits only the selected
order and its two neighbours at tolerance `1e-5` from an independent start.

## Robustness rerun

The StrataSCAN-only seven-family matrix contains 175 jobs: seven cases, five
sample sizes from 5,000 through 200,000, and five generated-data seeds. All 175
jobs completed. The conservative reference contains 165 successful paired
StrataSCAN jobs and 10 timeouts.

- paired median runtime ratio: `0.04424` (22.6x speedup);
- paired median pairwise-F1 change: `+0.00072`;
- paired mean pairwise-F1 change: `+0.02685`;
- all 10 conservative timeouts recovered.

The duplicate-profile guard was reevaluated on 11 applicable jobs. It removed
duplicate final signal profiles in every case. On imbalanced 20k seed 73,
pairwise F1 changed from 0.419 to 0.937 and extra fragments from 17 to 0. On
seed 101, pairwise F1 changed from 0.484 to 0.888 and extra fragments from 15
to 1.

Four pairwise-F1 regressions larger than 0.05 remain against the conservative
solver. They occur in rings or overlapping-density cases, not the imbalanced
family, and are retained as known limitations rather than hidden by the release
decision.

## Benchmark provenance

`benchmarks.run_campaign` is the single entry point for the release robustness,
replicated scaling, endpoint scaling, and ablation phases. The underlying
`benchmarks.run_benchmark` runner freezes input checksums and worker count,
rejects unsafe resume after source drift, and supports exact job-ID selection
for standalone partial reevaluations.

The 11 guarded resource reevaluations used four concurrent one-thread workers.
Their quality results are valid, but those individual resource measurements are
not a substitute for the serial v0.2.3 campaign defined for future execution.
