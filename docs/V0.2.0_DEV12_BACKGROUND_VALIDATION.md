# StrataSCAN 0.2.0-dev12 background-distribution validation

Status: historical experimental evidence for the 0.2.0 decision. No tested
continuous-rate background family was promoted. The released 0.2.0 estimator
uses the original `gamma_components` background formulation.

## Scope

This iteration changes only the probabilistic law of the single semantic
background state. Every signal state retains the same conditional Gamma shell
model and log-linear rate profile. For point `i`, shell `j`, and background
rank slope `b`, the shared conditional model is

```text
V_ij | lambda_i ~ Gamma(shape=a_j, rate=lambda_i * rank_j**b).
```

The comparison changes the latent background rate law `F` in
`lambda_i ~ F`:

1. `gamma_rate_mixture`: Gamma shape and rate;
2. `lognormal_rate_mixture`: normal location and scale on `log(lambda)`;
3. `inverse_gamma_rate_mixture`: inverse-Gamma shape and scale.

Each family has three free background parameters: two distribution parameters
and one rank slope. Signal-state parameters, semantic ICL, warm starts,
StrictCore extraction, graph construction, and all other controls are held
fixed. The lognormal marginal uses 24-node Gauss-Hermite quadrature with an
analytic gradient. The inverse-Gamma marginal is evaluated in closed form with
the modified Bessel function. Numerical M-steps are accepted only when they do
not reduce the weighted marginal likelihood.

The experimental options are exposed through `GammaMDLConfig` without changing
its default `background_distribution="gamma_components"`.

## Fixed-eight distribution screen

The reproducible driver is
`scripts/validate_background_distributions_dev12.py`, and the protocol is
`benchmarks/protocol.optimization-v0.2.0-dev12-background.json`. The first broad
screen used all seven synthetic families, uniform nulls in 2, 8, and 16
dimensions, `n=1,000`, development seed 23, one initialization, and candidate
counts 1 through 8. It is a development comparison, not locked evidence.

| Background law | Macro F1 | Pairwise F1 | Noise F1 | Boundary rate | Convergence | Null false-signal | Null false-cluster | Mean runtime |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Gamma rate | 0.7289 | 0.4771 | 0.7006 | 0.60 | 1.00 | 0.00 | 0.00 | 4.19 s |
| Lognormal rate | 0.6515 | 0.3416 | 0.5938 | 0.30 | 1.00 | 0.00 | 0.00 | 22.19 s |
| Inverse-Gamma rate | 0.6660 | 0.3794 | 0.5862 | 0.40 | 1.00 | 0.67 | 0.67 | 20.43 s |

The boundary rate counts selections within one state of the configured upper
bound and includes the seven synthetic and three null conditions. Quality
metrics average only the seven labelled synthetic datasets.

Inverse-Gamma is rejected for further work in this iteration. It produced
false signal and clusters on both the 8-D and 16-D uniform nulls, frequently
placed its shape at the finite-mean lower bound, and was slower and less
accurate than Gamma overall.

Lognormal is a useful control, not a replacement. It controlled every uniform
null and reduced component-bound pressure. It also improved the 8-D Gaussian
overlap and recovered the ultra-sparse case through the background topology
scan with no explicit signal state. However, it absorbed too much signal in
the imbalanced and continuously overlapping 16-D cases. Its scale also reached
the configured upper bound on the imbalanced case. The mean quality loss and
roughly fivefold runtime cost fail the promotion gates.

## Adaptive cap-to-16 check

Only the two null-safe families were retained for an adaptive search starting
at eight and expanding to at most 16 states.

| Case | Gamma states | Lognormal states |
|---|---:|---:|
| multidensity 2-D | 11 | 11 |
| ultra-sparse 16-D | 7 | 1 |
| overlapping-density 16-D | 10 | 10 |
| moons 2-D | 7 | 7 |
| rings 2-D | 6 | 6 |
| Gaussian overlap 8-D | 9 | 4 |
| Gaussian imbalanced 16-D | 16 | 6 |
| uniform null 2-D | 1 | 1 |
| uniform null 8-D | 1 | 1 |
| uniform null 16-D | 1 | 1 |

Each count includes the one explicit background state, so the nominal signal
stratum count is one less. A background-only state can still yield a cluster
when the separately coded background topology scan detects a finite
overdensity; this occurred for the ultra-sparse signal under the lognormal
model, but not on the uniform nulls.

| Background law | Macro F1 | Pairwise F1 | Noise F1 | Hard-bound rate | Convergence | Null false-cluster | Mean runtime |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Gamma rate | 0.7513 | 0.4778 | 0.6840 | 0.10 | 0.90 | 0.00 | 4.11 s |
| Lognormal rate | 0.6581 | 0.3506 | 0.5915 | 0.00 | 0.90 | 0.00 | 17.34 s |

Both non-convergence flags occurred on the selected 11-state multidensity fit
in this one-restart screen. Three-restart confirmation is required before any
solver conclusion.

## Decision

Changing only the latent background-rate distribution does not resolve the
original optimization problem:

- Gamma preserves more true signal but still uses signal components to
  approximate difficult density structure and reaches 16 states on the
  imbalanced case.
- Lognormal models background heterogeneity with fewer states, but its extra
  coverage is not identifiable from diffuse signal and therefore suppresses
  real clusters in important cases.
- Inverse-Gamma is insufficiently calibrated and fails high-dimensional nulls.

Therefore dev12 adds the families and their diagnostics for controlled
experiments but does not change the default background law. Moving the hard
cap to 16 remains useful as a diagnostic, not as the solution: it exposes an
interior optimum in several cases but also permits over-partitioning and leaves
one case at the new hard bound.

The subsequent 0.2.0 release fixes that unchanged discrete Gamma-component
default as the primary `StrataSCAN` algorithm; it does not promote the Gamma,
lognormal, or inverse-Gamma continuous-rate variants tested here.

The next background-specific formulation should model spatial accessibility or
inhomogeneous intensity, not only the marginal distribution of local rates.
It should be validated on inhomogeneous Cox-process and boundary-affected nulls
before touching the signal mixture or the public estimator.

Raw development evidence is in:

- `results/runs/optimization_dev12_background_fixed8_n1000_seed23/`;
- `results/runs/optimization_dev12_gamma_lognormal_adaptive16_n1000_seed23/`.
