# RQ4: Transfer to cytometry data

We evaluated all nine methods on 13 cytometry datasets (Levine, Mosmann, Nilsson, and ten Samusik samples) under the frozen `target-discovery-v1` contract. Levine, Nilsson, and Samusik use their declared type-marker panels; because Mosmann's reference target is an activated population, its primary panel uses seven type and seven state markers (14 total). Reference populations were matched to predicted clusters one-to-one by Hungarian optimization of pairwise F1. Macro target F1 was the primary endpoint; target purity, coverage, and discovery at purity $\geq 0.90$ and coverage $\geq 0.10$ were supporting endpoints. The matrix contained 117 attempted cells, of which 97 completed successfully.

StrataSCAN completed all 13 datasets and obtained median macro target F1 0.203 (IQR 0.190--0.248), the highest median among methods completing every dataset. It led Levine (0.190 versus 0.036 for the best successful baseline), Mosmann (0.565 versus 0.430), and Nilsson (0.517 versus 0.133). Its median target purity and coverage were 0.437 and 0.244. kNN+Leiden had the higher median over its 11 successful runs, 0.284 (IQR 0.265--0.302), and led all ten Samusik samples, but timed out on Levine and Mosmann. HDBSCAN and VDBSCAN-2007 also completed 11/13, OPTICS completed 12/13, and AMD-DBSCAN completed none; failed cells were treated as missing, never as zero-quality results.

The stricter discovery endpoint exposed a complementary trade-off. StrataSCAN produced qualifying high-purity discoveries in seven Samusik samples, whereas kNN+Leiden produced none and SNN-DBSCAN did so in six. Neither StrataSCAN nor SNN-DBSCAN qualified the Mosmann target because purity remained below 0.90; their mean discovery rates were 0.035 and 0.019. Thus, StrataSCAN leads the three non-Samusik datasets and combines complete execution with the strongest median recovery among full-completion methods, while kNN+Leiden remains stronger across the Samusik series.

**One-sentence answer.** With the biologically aligned 14-marker Mosmann panel, StrataSCAN leads Levine, Mosmann, and Nilsson and completes all 13 datasets, while kNN+Leiden leads the ten Samusik samples.
